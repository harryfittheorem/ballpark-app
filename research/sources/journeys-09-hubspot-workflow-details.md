Use the workflow details page to review your workflow history and performance. You can review a workflow's history to check past events and analyze its performance to measure effectiveness.

When troubleshooting specific records in your workflow, it's strongly recommended to[individual records' paths](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/workflow-enrollment-history?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=individual-records-paths&facet1=crm) directly in the workflow editor. You can also review the [conversion and performance data](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/workflow-enrollment-history?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=conversion-and-performance-data#review-the-enrollment-history-and-path-of-individual-records) for different actions from the workflow editor.

To understand your overall workflow performance, learn how to [analyze your workflows](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/analyze-workflow-performance-and-opportunities?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=analyze-your-workflows&facet1=crm) and use the [workflows health page](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/monitor-your-workflow-health?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=workflows-health-page&facet1=crm).

## Understand limitations and requirements

Permissions required [Super Admin](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/user-management/hubspot-user-permissions-guide?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=super-admin#super-admin) or [Workflows](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/user-management/hubspot-user-permissions-guide?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/chatflows/create-a-customer-agent&hubs_content-cta=Customer%20agent%20editor#crm-objects:~:text=Workflows%3A%20toggle%20the%C2%A0Workflows%20switch%20on%20to%20give%20the%20user%20access%20to%20workflows.) permissions are required to use the workflows tool.

After [creating your workflow](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/create-workflows?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=creating-your-workflow&facet1=crm) and turning it on, navigate to the workflow details page to view the events that have occurred in the workflow, including _action logs_ and _enrollment history_.

- HubSpot will store workflow data within the below time periods:

  - **90 days:** all workflow action log data will be stored. This includes high-level action log data such as object ID (which contact, company, deal, etc.), event type (enrollment, unenrollment, etc.), and action type will be stored.
  - **6 months:** historical data of enrollments will be retained in our system to enable workflow-based filters.
- When reviewing your workflow history, there is a 100,000 log limit for successful workflow executions per day.
  - This is calculated from midnight in the account's time zone.
  - Once the limit is exceeded:


    - Success and info logs will not be stored for the rest of the day.
    - Error logs will continue to appear.
    - Records will continue enrolling and executing normally through the workflow.

### Action logs

On the _Actions logs_ tab, view the past events within the workflow. The events are displayed in reverse chronological order, with the most recent events appearing at the top. In larger workflows, do note that contact enrollments and action activity may take time to appear.

01. In your HubSpot account, click **More**, then navigate to [**Automation** \> **Workflows**](https://app.hubspot.com/l/workflows?hubs_signup-url=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_signup-cta=automation-workflows&facet1=crm). If _More_ doesn't appear in your account, navigate to [**Automation** \> **Workflows**](https://app.hubspot.com/l/workflows?hubs_signup-url=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_signup-cta=automation-workflows&facet1=crm) directly.
02. Hover over the name of the workflow and click **More**. Then select **View details**.
03. At the top, click the **Action logs** tab.
04. The _Action logs_ table displays:


    - **\[Record\]:** the name of the record that links to its record page.
    - **Diagnose:** review details on why an action occurred.
    - **Action:** the step of the workflow that caused the event.
    - **Event:** the result of the action.
    - **Time:** when the action happened. The timestamp reflects the [account's default time zone](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account-management/change-your-language-and-region-settings?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=accounts-default-time-zone&facet1=crm).
05. By default, the _Actions logs_ display events for all event types. To filter events, in the upper left, click the **dropdown menus**:

    - **Date range:** filter by time period.
    - **Event:** filter by _All events_, _Sucesses_, _Information,_ or _Errors_. This can help to identify workflow issues, such as an email failing to send.
    - **Revisions:** filter by the version history of the workflow shown by timestamp.
    - **Actions in revision:** filter by a specific action in the workflow. A specific revision must be selected to use this filter, this filter won't work if the _Revisions_ filter is set to _All revisions_.
    - **Action types:** filter by the [workflow action type](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/choose-your-workflow-actions?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=workflow-action-type&facet1=crm).
06. To remove all filters, click **Clear filters**.
07. To export your action log data, in the upper right, click **Export** and select **Export action logs to .csv**.
08. To search for a specific record, in the upper right, click **Choose a trigger source**. In the dialog box, click the **Trigger source** dropdown menu, then search for and select a **specific record**.

    - For example, when searching for a record, you can use a ticket record's record ID or a contact record's email address.
    - If the record is currently enrolled or has ever been enrolled in the workflow, you can review all workflow events that occurred to the object.
    - If an expected record doesn't appear, you may want to try adjusting the selected date range.
09. To unenroll a record from the workflow, hover over an **event**, then click **Unenroll**. This option will only appear when the record is currently active in the workflow.
10. To view the workflow revision at the time of an event, hover over an **event**, then click **Event details**.


![workflows-details-page-event-details](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Knowledge_Base_2023-24-25/KB-Workflows/workflows-details-page-event-details.png?width=672&height=331&name=workflows-details-page-event-details.png)

- Click the **Timestamp** under _Workflow revision at time of event._
  - This timestamp reflects when the workflow was revised.
  - You'll also see the user who changed the workflow underneath the timestamp.

- On the workflow revision screen, view the enrollment conditions and actions set in the workflow in that version. You can click the **enrollment triggers** and **action** boxes to view the details of that version. The workflow cannot be edited from this view.

**Please note:** the workflow's revision history from the action logs do not include custom code actions, and settings such as unenrollment and suppression. These revisions are read-only and can't be restored. To restore a previous version of a workflow, learn how to [review and revert worfklow changes](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/view-workflow-changes?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=review-and-revert-worfklow-changes&facet1=crm) from the workflow editor.

### Enrollment history

In the _Enrollment history_ tab, view all workflow enrollments, including enrollments up to 6 months ago. It'll also display all actions that occurred for a record.

1. In your HubSpot account, click **More**, then navigate to [**Automation** \> **Workflows**](https://app.hubspot.com/l/workflows?hubs_signup-url=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_signup-cta=automation-workflows&facet1=crm). If _More_ doesn't appear in your account, navigate to [**Automation** \> **Workflows**](https://app.hubspot.com/l/workflows?hubs_signup-url=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_signup-cta=automation-workflows&facet1=crm) directly.
2. Hover over the name of the workflow and click **More**. Then select **View details**.
3. At the top, click the **Enrollment history** tab.
4. To filter your workflow enrollment history, in the upper left, click the dropdown menus:
   - **Date range:** filter by time period.
   - **Revisions:** filter by the version history of the workflow shown by timestamp.
5. To export your enrollment history data, in the upper right, click **Export** and select **Export action logs to .csv**.
6. To search for a specific record, in the upper right, click **Choose a trigger source**. In the dialog box, click the **Trigger source** dropdown menu, then search for and select a **specific record**.


   - If an expected record doesn't appear, you may want to try adjusting the selected date range.
7. To view the complete workflow history, hover over the **record** you want to see the history of, then click **Enrollment details**.
8. In the right panel, review the record's workflow history.
9. To view the details of an event, click the **name** of the event to expand it.

   - **Action**: the step of the workflow where the action occurred.
   - **Time:** the time that the event happened.
   - **Workflow revision at time of event**: the version of the workflow at the time of the event. You'll also see the user who changed the workflow. Click the **timestamp** to view the workflow layout at the time.

![workflows-details-page-enroll-history](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Knowledge_Base_2023-24-25/KB-Workflows/workflows-details-page-enroll-history.png?width=691&height=386&name=workflows-details-page-enroll-history.png)

### Issues

From the issues page, you can review an overall list of issues in your workflow that you may want to fix or ignore. In the workflow editor, you can then track[individual records' paths and errors](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/workflow-enrollment-history?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=individual-records-paths-and-errors&facet1=crm) to further troubleshoot any issues.

1. In your HubSpot account, click **More**, then navigate to [**Automation** \> **Workflows**](https://app.hubspot.com/l/workflows?hubs_signup-url=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_signup-cta=automation-workflows&facet1=crm). If _More_ doesn't appear in your account, navigate to [**Automation** \> **Workflows**](https://app.hubspot.com/l/workflows?hubs_signup-url=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_signup-cta=automation-workflows&facet1=crm) directly.
2. Hover over the name of the workflow and click **More**. Then select **View details**.
3. In the top right, click **Review workflow issues**.
4. **[Continue analyzing your workflow issues](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/troubleshoot-common-workflow-errors?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=continue-analyzing-your-workflow-issues#identify-workflows-with-errors)**.

## Analyze your workflow performance

On the _Performance_ tab, analyze the performance of your workflow with metrics such as enrollments and email trends.

1. In your HubSpot account, click **More**, then navigate to [**Automation** \> **Workflows**](https://app.hubspot.com/l/workflows?hubs_signup-url=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_signup-cta=automation-workflows&facet1=crm). If _More_ doesn't appear in your account, navigate to [**Automation** \> **Workflows**](https://app.hubspot.com/l/workflows?hubs_signup-url=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_signup-cta=automation-workflows&facet1=crm) directly.
2. Hover over the name of the workflow and click **More**. Then select **View details**.

### Top metrics

The _Top metrics_ report will show the following workflow data:

- **Total \[object-type\] enrolled:** the total number of times records were enrolled in the workflow. This includes records that may have enrolled more than once during re-enrollment.
- **Estimated unique \[object-type\] enrolled:** the total number of unique records enrolled in the workflow since creation. This does not include records that have re-enrolled.
- **7 day total enrollment:** the number of enrolled records in the past 7 days based on your account's timezone.


![workflows-details-page-performance](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Knowledge_Base_2023-24-25/KB-Workflows/workflows-details-page-performance.png?width=667&height=340&name=workflows-details-page-performance.png)

### Enrollment trends

The _Enrollment over time_ report shows the performance of enrolled records in the workflow in the selected time period:

- **\[Object-type\] enrolled:** the number of records currently enrolled in the workflow.
- **\[Object-type\] lost:** when a record is lost, this means that one of the following has occurred:
  - The record was [manually unenrolled](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manually-unenroll-objects-from-workflows?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=manually-unenrolled&facet1=crm).
  - The record met the [workflow's criteria](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/use-goals-in-contact-based-workflows?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=workflows-criteria&facet1=crm) and was not [sent a marketing email](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/choose-your-workflow-actions?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=sent-a-marketing-email#send-email-marketing-hub-and-service-hub-professional-and-enterprise-only).
  - The record was on a [suppression list](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manage-your-workflow-settings?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=suppression-list#suppression-lists-for-this-workflow).
  - The record was [removed by enrollment in another workflow](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manage-your-workflow-settings?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=removed-by-enrollment-in-another-workflow#when-contacts-enroll-in-this-workflow).
  - The record was removed by the [When a contact no longer meets the enrollment conditions, remove them from this workflow?](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/manage-your-workflow-settings?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=when-a-contact-no-longer-meets-the-enrollment-conditions-remove-them-from-this-workflow#when-contacts-enroll-in-this-workflow) unenrollment setting.
  - The records were lost due to contacts unsubscribing from an email, hard bouncing, or by marking an email as spam.
- **Completed workflow:** the number of records that completed the final action in the workflow.

To use the _Enrollment over time performance_ report:

1. Hover over a **data point** in the chart to view a numerical breakdown of each metric.
2. To select or compare different time periods:
   - In the top right, click **Actions**, then select **Edit**.
   - In the dialog box, you can update the following:
     - **Date range:** select a new custom **date range**.
     - **Data frequency:** select from a **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly** data frequency.
     - **Compare to:** select the **checkbox** to compare your data to a different time period. Then, click the **dropdown menu** and select **Last week**, **Last month**, **Last quarter**,or **Last year**.

- After updating your time period, at the bottom, click **Save**.

![workflows-details-page-enroll-trends](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Knowledge_Base_2023-24-25/KB-Workflows/workflows-details-page-enroll-trends.png?width=611&height=431&name=workflows-details-page-enroll-trends.png)

### Email trends ( _Marketing Hub Professional_ and _Enterprise_ only)

Workflows with [automated marketing emails](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/marketing-email/create-automated-emails-to-use-in-workflows?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=automated-marketing-emails&facet1=crm) that were sent with a [_Send email_ action](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/choose-your-workflow-actions?hubs_content=knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/understand-your-workflow-details-page&hubs_content-cta=send-email-action#send-email) will have the following email performance report:

- **Emails in workflow:** this report shows all marketing emails sent in the workflow. You can review the the percentage of contacts who: skipped, were sent, delivered, opened, and clicked the email. Send counts will only include contacts that reached the _Send email_ action. Contacts waiting in a delay before the action will not be included. If the _Send email_ action was deleted or replaced, sends from the earlier action will not be counted toward the new action's metrics.


  - To sort the report by a specific metric, click the corresponding **column header**.
  - To select a different time period, in the top right, click the **date pickers** and set a date range.
  - To view raw counts instead of rates (percentages), in the top right, click the **View as** dropdown menu.
