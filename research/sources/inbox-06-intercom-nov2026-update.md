When a customer writes in more than once about the same thing, you used to merge those conversations one at a time. Now you can merge up to 100 into a single conversation in one step.

- **From a conversation** — open Merge into…, choose the primary conversation that stays open, then select any others to merge into it
- **From your inbox** — select the conversations you want to merge, choose Merge into…, then pick which one is the primary
- **After merging** — the primary conversation shows a single card listing every merged conversation, grouped by what the customer asked about
