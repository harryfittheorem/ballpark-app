# A new object trigger that meets more of your needs

November 2, 2026  Messaging

The new **Object** trigger starts a journey when a person, their relationship, and the related object meet all of your conditions, in any order. Learn more about the new trigger and how it improves upon the classic triggers.

![A new object trigger that meets more of your needs](https://docs.customer.io/images/release-notes/trigger-object-new.png)

The [Object trigger](https://docs.customer.io/messaging/send/automations/object-triggers/#object-trigger) replaces the separate trigger, filter, and audience conditions of the classic triggers with one set of conditions. With the new trigger, you’ll see a number of enhancements:

- **Order no longer matters.** Classic triggers check your conditions only at the moment an object or relationship changes, so a person who meets your audience conditions later misses the automation. With the Object trigger, people enter when all conditions are true.
- **More flexible conditions.** Group conditions with _OR_ and nest them, instead of a single list joined by _AND_.
- **Control over re-entry.** Choose **One time** so each relationship enters once, or **Every re-match**. Classic triggers always use every re-match.
- **People exit when they stop matching trigger conditions.** By default, people leave the automation when the trigger conditions are no longer true.

Automations running on the classic triggers continue to run as before.
