# Get real-time conversational assistance in your inbox with Copilot

Edited 16 days ago

## Overview

Copilotis your always-on AI assistant, helping you quickly understand what matters most in every customer interaction. It brings together context, history, and knowledge across conversations to summarize requests, surface key details, and guide next steps— so you can move from questions to confident action without manual searching.

With Copilot, you can:

- Get instant answers about your customer, their history, and what to do next

- Summarize conversations and surface relevant details instantly

- Find similar past conversations and knowledge articles automatically

- Generate replies from past conversations, knowledge base articles, and external sources

- Draft, refine, and iterate on replies with custom prompts


Learn more in our [Front Academy Copilot](https://academy.front.com/app/courses/67396e26-9179-4a6b-b6a9-6ea1d7cfa4c1) course.

* * *

## Prerequisites

You must have the [Copilot](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2344960) add-on or have Copilot included in your plan.

Copilot is designed to work off-the-shelf; no complex setup necessary! However, we recommend that admins follow these steps to help Copilot produce high quality answers:

1. **Connect important shared inboxes to AI.** Navigate to workspace settings, then select _Front AI_ to enable and configure inboxes. By connecting an inbox to AI, Copilot is able to provide higher quality answers from your previous conversations.

2. **Connect your knowledge base to Front AI.** Copilot automatically has access to your Front knowledge base content. We also recommend connecting additional external knowledge sources. [See this article to learn more](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4614336).

3. **Enable the _Compose_ company AI setting** to use custom prompts when composing with AI.

## Using the Copilot sidebar

**Step 1**

Navigate to a conversation, then click the Copilot icon next to the comment bar. You can also hover over the icon to see a list of suggested quick actions.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/ff50c845-6b54-43c3-8c14-ca77adcaa6d3.png)

**Step 2**

In the Copilot sidebar, type your question in the text box, then click send or press enter on your keyboard.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/aa5ac2ef-adae-43da-907b-3423ee85cd8f.png)

Other actions you can take:

- Drag and drop conversations into the sidebar to ask AI about multiple conversations at once.

- Use the plus icon (+) to add attachments, mention resources or teammates, or use skills.

- Use the settings icon to select the knowledge sources you want Copilot to use to generate answers, manage routines, or manage skills. Select the _Web search_ knowledge source to search the web directly from Copilot.


![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/c296260d-e47d-4747-acef-c2eea0835c5c.png)

- At the top of the panel, you can:

  - Search previous chats using the downward caret icon at the top left of the panel

  - Start a new chat using the pencil icon

  - Select between using Copilot in a _Floating_ comment bar or using the right-hand _Sidebar_

  - Pin the chat using the _Stay on this chat_ toggle

  - Open Copilot in full screen using the expanding arrows icon

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/ebda8747-1c18-4ccb-9181-8498ce682a00.png)

In the _Floating_ comment bar mode, you can click the Copilot icon to toggle between Copilot and the standard comment composer.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/4836b9f4-2917-4d0e-8d8b-02f93e2f6f75.png)

**Step 3**

Copilot will generate an answer in the sidebar. From this sidebar, you can also:

- View which activities and knowledge sources Copilot used to create the response

- Ask follow up questions

- Click the pencil icon at the top to start a new inquiry


![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/78d9235c-5bcd-45b4-895b-921396bd07ec.png)

## Skills

For prompts you frequently ask Copilot, skills let you create shortcuts so you can get answers and take actions faster.

### Creating skills

**Step 1**

Hover over the Copilot icon, then select _New skill_.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/eb160a8b-f45d-4653-babf-f6f6531a128d.png)

You can also navigate to the Copilot icon in the navigation menu, select _Skills_, then click _Create_. To create a skill using Copilot chat, use the text box.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/6b06f5c2-24c6-43f7-8c0e-83991f48a154.png)

**Step 2**

In this example, we’ll manually create a skill. Fill in the following:

- **Name:** Internal name of the skill

- **Description:** Explain when to use the skill or what it does

- **Color or emoji:** Select a color or emoji for the skill

- **Pin as quick action:** Check the box if you want to save the skill as a quick action

- **Share with teammates** (company admins only):Select the teammates who can view and use the skill. Leave the field blank to create a private skill. If a skill is shared with teammates, it cannot be made private again. All company admins can edit shared skills.

- **Instructions:** Describe the action you'd like Copilot to take


Click _Create_.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/a6583b4f-f3ac-41fb-9df0-fe3585240de7.png)

**Step 3**

Hover over the Copilot icon or select it from the shortcuts above the comment bar to use it. If you created a shared skill, your teammates must pin the skill to see it as a shortcut.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/4d136496-bab3-43b2-86ed-730ff0313f67.png)

### Editing or deleting skills

**Step 1**

Click the Copilot icon next to the comment composer to open the sidebar, or click the Copilot icon in the navigation menu to view Copilot in fullscreen mode.

**Step 2**

In the sidebar, click the settings icon, then select _Skills_.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/ab338264-9beb-48b0-8aa4-2c8db57e27af.png)

In fullscreen mode, select _Skills_ in the sidebar.

**Step 3**

Hover over the skill you'd like to edit, click the three-dot menu, then select _Edit_ or _Delete_, depending on the action you want to take. You can also pin/unpin the skill using the push pin icon.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/ae2ef346-c6e4-4fb4-a11b-1ff1442a467f.png)

**Step 4**

If editing, you'll navigate to the editor to make your changes. If deleting, you'll see a pop-up asking you to confirm the deletion.

### Pinned skills

Pin your most frequently used skills for quick access. Up to 3 skills are visible above the comment bar, and up to 10 skills can be set as quick actions when hovering over the Copilot icon.

**Step 1**

Open any conversation. Your pinned skills appear as shortcuts above the comment bar, or when you hover over the Copilot icon.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/49dfbf56-7b1d-468f-b170-90f14d132367.png)

**Step 2**

Select a skill shortcut to run it. Copilot opens in the right-hand panel and runs the skill against the current conversation.

**Step 3**

If the number of pinned skills exceeds what fits above the comment bar, click the overflow shortcut at the end of the row to open a dropdown. The dropdown lists all of your personal skills, both pinned and unpinned.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/b7118692-6a52-4629-90e8-ff91221b6b0f.png)

**Step 4**

To change which skills appear as shortcuts, navigate to your skills settings and pin or unpin them.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/91f1e5de-1331-440a-a23f-934912be5341.png)

## Bestpractices

### Example questions

Please feel free to ask Copilot any range of questions - Copilot is designed to be helpful across a range of different workflows.

Copilot searches conversations and knowledge sources to answer questions. It also responds to ‘filters’ you give it via the prompts you write. ‘@mentioning’ a teammate, contact, shared inbox, tag, etc. may help you get even more specific responses.

Some examples of questions you may want to ask Copilot include:

**Understanding and summarizing conversations**

- “What is this customer asking for?”

- “Summarize this conversation so far.” or “Summarize what ‘@teammate’ said?”

- “What has the customer already tried or shared?”


**Searching within and across conversations**

- “Did the customer mention an order number or deadline?”

- “Find other conversations related to this issue.”

- “Show similar conversations we’ve handled before.”


**Finding conversations by customer, tag, app object, or criteria**

- “Show me past conversations with this @customer or @account.”

- “Find conversations mentioning refunds (or another topic).”

- “Find conversations related to order @#12345.”

- "What meetings do I have with @account this week?"


**Sentiment-based insights**

- “Show conversations where customers were frustrated or unhappy in @inbox or @view.”

- “Are there recent complaints about \[feature name\]?”


**Process, policy, and how-to questions**

_Note: Requires knowledge source with this information to be connected to Front as an AI knowledge source. Go to Front AI, then Knowledge sources to add._

- “What is our policy on refunds?”

- “What’s the process for handling this request?”

- “How do I do this?”


**Drafting replies and comments**

- “Draft a reply to the customer explaining the setup process”

- “Draft a comment with context on the issue to handoff to technical support”


### Other supported resources

- **Accounts and contacts:** Click _Summarize_ in the [account details page](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2285#account_details) or [_Relationships_ plugin](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2226#contact_header) to generate insights on conversations from an account or contact.

- **Analytics:** Copilot answers metric questions, returns tables, supports drill-down, CSV export, and filters by inbox, channel, tag, workspace and teammate.

- **Message templates:** Use Copilot to search through your message templates and edit them as needed.


### Multiple chats

Copilot can answer multiple questions at the same time. Use the _New chat_ icon at the top of the panel to ask Copilot another question while it thinks through the first one. Feel free to ask your questions, move on to other tasks, and return to both chats at your convenience.

### Tips and tricks

If you’re trying to search for a particular knowledge base article, but aren’t seeing results, double check that your knowledge base is connected in your workspace AI settings. If you haven’t connected your knowledge base before, we have a [guide](https://help.front.com/en/articles/4614336) to help walk you through it!

Copilot starts by looking at the conversation you’re currently on, and then looking at sources related to that conversation. If you’re looking for something more specific, try the following:

**@mentions**

You can ‘@mention’ any teammate, contact, shared inbox, tag, app object, view, custom field, or conversation to make your question more specific. This will instruct Copilot to specifically look at information related to this object when answering your question.

**Multi-select**

You can also select multiple conversations at the same time and ask Copilot about them. Copilot will then start by looking at all selected conversations to answer your question.

_If none of the following options work, please use the 👍 thumbs up / 👎 thumbs down feature to let us know! We’re continuing to make investments here and your specific feedback is helpful in understanding what we can do better._

- Conversation assignee

## Using Copilot to draft replies

Use the spacebar shortcut when the composer is empty, or click the _Compose with Copilot_ icon to draft new replies, refine existing text, or iterate multiple times with custom prompts.

Example prompts:

- Draft a helpful follow-up when a customer hasn’t replied in a few days.

- Thank a customer for feedback and explain how it helps.

- Add structure with bullets or numbered steps.

- Summarize the issue in one line at the top.


[See this article](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2003#faq) to learn more about the information Copilot uses to generate responses.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/4776c657-182f-4143-a043-14234e84005b.gif)

For existing messages, you’ll see preset options as well as the text field for writing custom prompts.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/8851af2c-619c-4310-8607-dc64bcc1b3d4.png)

### Drafting: Custom instructions

In the composer, click the gear icon, then select _Instructions_ to set up personal guidelines that are automatically applied to every Copilot draft.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/96583a83-26d8-41d2-bd46-5f862634cc70.png)

Check the box next to _Include custom instructions_, then enter your guidelines in the text field, up to 2000 characters. You can uncheck the box at any time to stop using custom instructions.

Once enabled and saved, these instructions will apply to all Copilot-composed drafts.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/6d90ce8c-3be8-4fe3-8e53-83f7c86e0d8a.png)

### Drafting: Quick actions

In the composer, click the gear icon, then select _Quick actions_ to set up shortcuts for prompts you frequently use.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/a96d7cb5-4b10-4161-82ec-b99bea1295bf.png)

Select _Create quick action_, then enter a label and the prompt you want to run, up to 2000 characters. You can return your quick action settings at any time to edit or remove them.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/2a8e6ae1-061f-4150-b727-185e0fdbe3fa.png)

Once created, you can trigger the quick action via a single click in the composer.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/aa061ffa-6663-4736-b673-b795e8db7368.png)

## FAQ

### What conversations can Copilot be used on?

Copilot can be used on all Front conversations, including conversations in shared and individual inboxes, discussions, and tasks.

### What conversations and sources does Copilot have access to?

General rule of thumb: Copilot has the same permissions as the teammate using it.

Copilot can access a teammate’s individual (personal) inbox, as well as conversations and AI-connected knowledge sources within the shared workspaces they belong to.

- It cannot access other teammates’ individual inboxes or any conversations or knowledge sources in workspaces the teammate is not assigned to.

- Company admins must be added as a member of a workspace if they need Copilot to access resources from that workspace.


### Can Copilot read attachments?

Yes. Copilot can answer questions related to both attachments and inline images in messages, in addition to attachments added via _Add images or files_ in the plus icon (+).

### Are there rate limits?

Yes. Copilot has the following limits:

- Copilot sidebar: Users may encounter rate limits for unusually high activity.

- Copilot drafting: 200 requests per user per day. Each compose action or transformation counts as a request.


These limits are separate from Front’s other AI features. [See here](https://help.front.com/en/articles/3506304#front_ai_rate_limits) to review other rate limits.

### Can I disable Copilot?

Yes. Please contact our Support team to disable the feature for your Front instance.

### Are my Copilot instructions and quick actions available on all my devices?

Yes. Copilot custom instructions and quick actions are personal settings saved to your account, and are synced across your sessions and devices. You cannot share custom instructions and quick actions you've created with other teammates.

### How do I hide the skills shortcuts above the comment bar?

To hide the skills shortcuts above the comment bar AND when hovering over the Copilot icon, open the Copilot panel, click the _Settings_ icon, then select _Skills_.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/d9e94654-2a02-4a9d-9903-47de582b24d8.png)

Hover over the skills you want to hide, then click the pin icon to unpin it.

![](https://usw1.frontkb-cdn.com/attachments/11/1/be2a246b-8353-4e66-95b8-672565c0a94b.png)

### Are pinned skill shortcuts available on mobile?

Not at this time. It is not currently possible to use skill shortcuts on the mobile app.
