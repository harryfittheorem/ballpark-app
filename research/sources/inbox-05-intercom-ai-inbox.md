# Inbox AI features

## Copilot

With [Copilot](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/9121374-fin-ai-copilot-explained) seamlessly integrated into your inbox, you can get instant, expert answers sourced from your team's conversation history, internal articles, public articles, and external content. Teammates can get support with:

- Finding answers to customer queries in one place.

- Troubleshooting complex issues.

- Training and onboarding processes and material.

- Working more efficiently in the inbox.

## AI Compose

Easily rephrase answers, adjust to your tone, translate replies or fix spelling.

- Expand or Rephrase - write shorthand customer responses and let the AI generate a complete suggested or rephrased message.

- Change tone - automatically change the tone of the message you’ve written to sound more like your own tone, be more casual/friendly or more formal depending on what suits your business or the situation. The system searches through your previous replies, It selects relevant examples of how you have responded in the past—capturing your unique style, preferred phrases, and emoji usage. This generates a new reply that matches the content you want to send, but in your personal tone and style

- Fix grammar - fix your grammar and spelling mistakes, to ensure your replies are always flawless.

- Translate - translate your message into another language if you're handling a query in a different language.

## AI Summarize

Create an AI-generated summary of a customer conversation or ticket. To pull up the AI Compose options quickly, just hit **` ⌘ J`** / **` Ctrl J`.** Teammates can choose to edit a summary first before adding the **Note** to the conversation, or add the **Note** straight away.

AI Summarize requires the **Advanced plan** or above. To automatically generate summaries via Workflows, the **Expert plan** is required.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/904291657/c22120525c6f575551a532ce/Summarise.gif?expires=1795147200&signature=885cc293c3107d4c465ffee311e4d167eb875a5a89fd5ad7d0c4fef272beb2d1&req=fSAjFMB%2Fm4RYFb4f3HP0gINhiuDMdoHIJHDWFB5bFYO1Yjs8g8w8N6rnOQlW%0AsKQtzPzymHZvagCYKw%3D%3D%0A)

Summaries can also be triggered automatically using the **Add summary note** workflow action, available on the **Expert plan**. [Learn how to set up AI summary Workflows](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8414839-trigger-ai-conversation-summaries-using-workflows).

**Note:** By default, AI summary notes are generated in the language detected from the conversation. If you’d like summaries to always generate in your workspace’s default language instead, contact our support team — they can enable this for your workspace.

## Human in the Loop

When Fin needs an approval, a piece of information or a decision, Fin assigns the conversation to you and asks. You'll see **Fin's original draft** and an **Answer Fin's question** panel that says exactly what Fin needs. **You make one decision, and Fin does the rest.** You're briefing Fin, not replying to a ticket. Waiting questions are listed under **Fin for Service › Needs teammate input** in the Inbox.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/tx2p130c/2814009937/d922c9675bdbfdbd050979506ebe/CleanShot+2026-11-16+at+11_05_24%402x.png?expires=1795147200&signature=4c6c0be23b0b07cc8134a089fc16b264f37a4002d700f04567f3fd3a43ae0bfa&req=digmEsl%2BlIhcXvMW1HO4zUUmZT9i%2B3lt5C3Jx9B2WDePKpHWyo%2Bv3KHEk95d%0AoMNWMNg05R5whqLiJx0%3D%0A)

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Option** | **When** | **What happens** |
| **Approve original solution** | The draft is right | Fin sends the draft |
| **Give Fin an answer** | Fin is missing a fact or decision, or the draft needs changing | Fin writes a new draft. Click **Approve** to send it, or **Ask Fin to iterate** to get another one. If the rule is set to Get back to customers right away, Fin sends the new draft without showing you. |
| **Ask Fin to iterate** | Fin's new draft isn't quite right | Tell Fin what to change; Fin writes another draft |
| **Other options › Take over from Fin** | Fin can't finish (an action only you can do, or the topic is too sensitive) | Pick a reason; Fin steps out and the conversation is yours, with the full context |

### Best practices

- **An answer beats taking over.** If one sentence would fix the draft, give Fin the answer. An answer costs you a sentence instead of the whole reply, and taking over without an answer leaves Fin nothing to learn from. Take over when the conversation really needs you: an action only you can do, or a customer who needs a person.

- **A good answer** is short and factual, and gives Fin the thing only you can see or decide: "Refund of RM399 issued on 9 Oct to the Visa ending 4421, arrives by 16 Oct." or "Doesn't qualify under the policy, but approve a goodwill refund of the missed night." Give the decision, not the wording. Then read Fin's new draft before you click **Approve**: Fin combines your answer with your content, and can smooth over an exception (for example writing "qualifies for a refund" when you said goodwill). Click **Ask Fin to iterate** and say what to change, or click **Edit** on your answer.

- **Your answers teach Fin.** Every answer shows your admin what Fin is missing (a system Fin can't see, a policy detail, a decision rule). Over time that means Fin asks you less, not more.

- **Watch the countdown.** On chat, the question shows how long you have left (for example "4m"). When time runs out, Fin follows the rule's Timeout setting: Fin sends its latest draft and carries on, or hands the conversation to someone else. On email, answer before the time set on the rule runs out.

- **When you take over**, pick the reason that fits: Fin's answer isn't right, the tone or wording isn't right, too sensitive for Fin, too complex for Fin, you need time to look into it, it's faster to reply yourself, or other. Your admin uses these reasons to improve Fin.


**Avoid:**

- ❌ Typing the whole reply in the answer box. Give the facts.

- ❌ Writing "I'm taking this over" in the answer box. Click **Other options › Take over from Fin**.

- ❌ Taking over when a sentence would do.

- ❌ Asking Fin to do what only you can (security checks, internal tools). Do the task, then tell Fin the result.


**Good to know:**

- If the customer asks something else, Fin may ask you again on the same conversation, and the new question comes straight to you.

- When the answer is sent, Fin unassigns you and carries on with the conversation.

- Your answers help your admin see what Fin needs to learn, so Fin asks less over time.

- Fin's question starts with a short summary of the conversation, and the question itself is in bold.

- When you answer or approve, the customer sees "Reviewed by \[your first name\]" under Fin's reply (web Messenger and email; not yet in iOS or Android apps).


**Tip:** [Read the full Human in the Loop guide](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/16198358-ask-a-teammate-for-help-with-fin-s-replies-human-in-the-loop).

# Get started

To enable Inbox AI features, navigate to your workspace [Settings > AI & Automation > Inbox AI](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/settings/ai-automation/helpdesk-ai) and toggle the desired features on.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/tx2p130c/1645036125/f5e8010d10253007dec0ab6235fe/CleanShot+2025-07-30+at+10_40_38%402x.png?expires=1795147200&signature=2d1535f0426cb3a464b52af419413abaee52061c35f1ed7487738d2eac8a0f51&req=dSYjE8l9m4BdXPMW1HO4zSHQa6mMTznR45ga8e9szb3c9B8dPVo%2FI1uiN6mz%0AlGcYiV9lVJ04cT2hVqk%3D%0A)

AI Compose & Summarize will be enabled automatically for new customers who joined since September 12, 2023.

### How these features are being used by Support teams

- **Copilot** \- Teammates are finding answers and troubleshooting customer queries, and getting support while onboarding with all resources available in one place.

- **Rephrase** \- Support reps are using the 'Rephrase' feature to add some variety into their replies and macros. This makes their responses more personal, as they are also well adjusted to their personal tone. They use it to make replies more fun and engaging. Also, they’ll use it when they feel like their response is a little awkward. It gives them a new way to say the same thing with clarity.

- **Make more formal** \- Some teammates like using the ‘Make more formal’ feature because it helps them tune-up their language in cases where that’s appropriate.

- **Summarize -** Support reps are using 'Summarize' when handing over conversations or escalating to a different team and bringing them up to speed quickly. The summaries help other teams get caught up with the specifics efficiently and allows them to determine next steps quicker. They also use it to summarize very long conversations, helping reps get up to speed quicker.


## Metadata in the Intercom API

AI summaries are also available via the Intercom API. To see the AI summaries, search for an individual conversation at this [endpoint](https://developers.intercom.com/docs/references/rest-api/api.intercom.io/Conversations/retrieveConversation/), and AI summaries should appear as a conversation part type of `conversation_summary`.

**Note:** Inbox AI features will work with multiple languages based on the 'Language' attribute of the conversation.

For example, if the detected locale of a customer is Brazil, the conversation language will be set to Portuguese and the summary will be translated accordingly. If detection fails, the workspace's default language is used as a fallback. For AI Summarize specifically, it's also possible to configure summaries to always generate in your workspace's default language — contact Intercom support to enable this for your workspace. This option applies to AI Summarize only and doesn't affect other inbox AI features such as AI Compose.

This is based on the following criteria:

1. The language automatically detected in the initial customer message of the conversation, based on your Messenger's supported languages.

2. If no language is detected in the initial customer message, it will use the customer's current Messenger location. If there is no Messenger location for the customer, it will use the workspace's location.

# FAQs

### Why didn't my Workflow summary get posted?

This applies to the **Add summary note** workflow step only — the manual inbox Summarize button is not subject to these conditions.

Check the following:

- **Plan:** The workflow step requires the **Expert plan**. On the Advanced plan, only the manual inbox button is available.

- **Message count:** The conversation must have more than 3 messages.

- **Conversation rate limit:** No more than 2 summaries can be posted to the same conversation per hour.

- **Workspace rate limit:** No more than 1,500 summaries can be generated across your workspace per hour.

- **AI Summarize enabled:** Confirm AI Summarize is toggled on in [Settings > AI & Automation > Inbox AI](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/settings/ai-automation/helpdesk-ai).


Phone call conversations use a dedicated summary format and are not handled by this workflow step.

### Can Fin summarize all tickets and conversations from a specific customer or person?

AI Summarize can't be used to summarize all conversations and tickets from one specific user in the inbox.

AI Summarize can only be used on a per-conversation or per-ticket basis, either by performing the action manually from the inbox or adding the action within a workflow.

### Which languages does the AI translate feature support?

Croatian, Danish, English, French, German, Hebrew, Hindi, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, and Turkish.

### Are there any additional costs to using these features?

Copilot is available with included usage on all plans. Unlimited usage can be given to individual teammates as an add-on to your subscription. [Learn more](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/9121384-fin-ai-copilot-included-and-unlimited-usage).

AI Compose 'Adjust my Tone' and AI Autofill are only available on some pricing plans. AI Summarize is available on the **Advanced plan** and above for the manual inbox button, and requires the **Expert plan** for the Workflow 'Add summary note' action. For additional information, visit the [Additional Product Terms](https://www.intercom.com/legal/terms-and-policies/additional-product-terms).

### How is Intercom handling privacy/data security?

You will need to agree to your data being sent to OpenAI as a subprocessor. Learn more in the [Additional Product Terms](https://www.intercom.com/legal/terms-and-policies/additional-product-terms).

### What safeguards are in place to avoid abuse/misuse?

Inbox AI features are agent-facing rather than customer-facing, and require human input/review.

### Are there additional legal terms I need to agree to in order to use the features?

Yes, all Inbox AI participation is subject to the [Additional Product Terms](https://www.intercom.com/legal/terms-and-policies/additional-product-terms).

### What are smart replies?

Smart replies are AI-powered suggested greetings in the inbox composer that teammates can accept/reject using the **` tab`** key. They can't be manually edited, but are based on common greetings teammates have used in the past.

You'll recognize smart replies as the gray placeholder text that appears in the inbox composer when you start a new reply — for example, displaying a suggested greeting like _"Hi \[customer name\], thank you for reaching out"_.

Press **` tab`** to accept the suggestion, or press **` Esc`** (or continue typing) to dismiss it.

**Note:**

- Smart replies are automatically enabled for all workspaces and can't be toggled on/off from Settings.


### How do I turn off the grey text suggestions in my inbox composer?

Unfortunately, you cannot turn off the grey text suggestions called **Smart replies** in your inbox composer. Smart replies are automatically enabled for all workspaces and cannot be toggled on or off from Settings. These AI-powered greeting suggestions will continue to appear when you start typing new messages. You can simply ignore the suggestions by continuing to type your own message—they will disappear as you write.

### Why does text from a macro keep appearing as grey text in my composer?

Grey text that keeps appearing in your inbox composer, even when it matches one of your macros, is a smart reply, not a macro. Smart replies are suggested for each teammate based on greetings and replies they've sent before, so if you've often started replies with the same wording (for example, by using a macro), that wording can come back as a smart reply suggestion.

Deleting the macro or changing who it's available to won't stop the smart reply, and nothing in your macro settings needs to change. Press **` Esc`** or keep typing to dismiss the smart reply, or press **` tab`** to accept it.
