# What emails and text messages are allowed under Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation?

Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) was put in place to reduce the number of unsolicited emails and text messages that Canadians receive and to create a safer online space.

Under CASL, businesses located in Canada can send you commercial messages if they have an “existing business relationship” with you or you have explicitly provided consent. However, they must include an unsubscribe option in each message. (Some emails and texts, such as those sent by political parties or charities soliciting donations, are not subject to CASL.)

If these emails or text messages bother you, let the sender know by unsubscribing, or contact them and ask to be removed from their distribution list.

## How to report spam

Canadians can report spam (unsolicited emails and texts) to the [Spam Reporting Centre](https://www.fightspam.gc.ca/eic/site/030.nsf/frm-eng/MMCN-9EZV6S).

Important: If you have been threatened, are the victim of a crime, or fear for someone’s safety, **contact your local police**.
