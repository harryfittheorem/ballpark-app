# Infinite Hitting — Athlete App Specification

**Version:** 0.3
**Status:** Design prototype / pre-development spec
**Owner:** FITT Space Coaching / MyJSC OS
**Target client:** Infinite Hitting (~20 US locations, baseball training)
**Target users:** Youth athletes (8U–18U) and their parents
**Companion to:** Coach app, Front Desk/Studio Management app, Admin Console

---

## 1. Product Vision

The Infinite Hitting Athlete App is the **player-facing surface** of a vertical SaaS platform purpose-built for baseball training studios. It replaces the generic Mindbody/booking-app experience with something an 11-year-old actually wants to open every day — gamified, video-first, and tightly coupled to in-cage capture systems (HitTrax, Rapsodo, Blast).

The thesis: **a kid who watches their own swings, races a leaderboard, and earns points toward a custom bat is a kid who shows up, does homework, and renews.** Retention and LTV are won inside this app, not in the CRM.

### Core promises to the athlete

1. **Every swing you take in the cage shows up on your phone within minutes** — with metrics, ranked, shareable.
2. **Your coach can reach you between sessions** — via text, video markup, and homework drills that earn points.
3. **Your effort earns real things** — apparel, free sessions, camp entries, custom equipment.
4. **You can see where you stack up** — locally, regionally, nationally, by age group.

### Core promises to the parent

1. Visibility into what their kid is doing and how they're progressing.
2. The ability to send game footage to the coach without a separate texting workflow.
3. A reason to keep paying that isn't just "we have a session Tuesday."

### Core promises to Infinite Hitting (the operator)

1. A network effect: PRs and share cards distribute the brand on social organically.
2. A retention moat: kids and parents don't leave platforms they're emotionally invested in.
3. A data layer: every swing, every assignment, every redemption is structured and queryable.
4. A displacement story vs. Mindbody: Mindbody books sessions; this *is* the training relationship.

## 2. Strategic Context

### Why this exists

Infinite Hitting currently uses a stack that includes Mindbody (or similar) for scheduling/billing, scattered text threads for coach↔parent communication, and HitTrax/Rapsodo systems whose data lives on cage-side terminals and rarely makes it back to the athlete. There is no consumer-grade, branded surface that ties the whole experience together.

This app is the centerpiece of a vertical SaaS pitch modeled loosely on **GolfTec's** approach: own the player relationship, own the data, own the brand. Mindbody is the incumbent to displace.

### Adjacent systems (already in flight or planned)

- **Coach App** — assignment creation, video markup, swing review queue, athlete roster
- **Front Desk / Studio OS** — scheduling, check-ins, billing, Stripe Connect aggregator (multi-location volume = better processing rates; Stripe/Adyen/Braintree/Finix outreach in progress)
- **Admin / HQ Console** — multi-location analytics, leaderboard management, reward catalog management, franchise reporting
- **Capture integrations** — HitTrax, Rapsodo, Blast Motion (swing data ingest)
- **Payment processing** — Stripe Connect with aggregated volume strategy
- **GoHighLevel** — CRM/marketing automation layer (FITT Space Coaching agency stack)

### Build context

- Built on the **MyJSC OS** platform (FITT Space Coaching's proprietary ops system)
- Architected for multi-tenant from day one (Infinite Hitting is the anchor tenant; product is white-labelable for other vertical SaaS deployments)
- Owner has nearly 20 years of software dev experience and is moving development to a local **Claude Code** environment with CLAUDE.md / .claude/ structure / MCP servers / sub-agents / skills

## 3. Brand & Design System

### Voice

Confident, clean, slightly aspirational — closer to a premium athletic brand (Nike, Vuori) than a kids' app. The 11-year-old user is treated as a real athlete, not a child. Copy is short, direct, and uses sport vocabulary ("PR", "exit velo", "session", "rep") naturally.

### Visual identity

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `dark` | `#2D2B2A` | Card backgrounds, secondary surfaces |
| `darker` | `#1C1B1A` | Mid-depth backgrounds |
| `darkest` | `#0F0E0E` | App background |
| `gold` | `#F1E5AD` | Primary accent, CTAs, highlights, PR badges |
| `goldBright` | `#E8D89A` | Gradient companion to gold |
| `goldDeep` | `#B8A268` | Pressed/dimmed gold |
| `cream` | `#FAF6E8` | Body text on dark |
| `white` | `#FFFFFF` | Headings on dark |
| `muted` | `#6B6560` | Secondary text |
| `border` | `#3A3836` | Hairlines |
| `borderGold` | `rgba(241, 229, 173, 0.2)` | Featured-card hairlines |
| `success` | `#A8C67F` | Confirmations |
| `warning` | `#E8A747` | Attention |
| `danger` | `#C86B5C` | Negative states / record indicators |

### Typography

- **Display:** Oswald / Bebas Neue / Impact — used for all-caps headlines, stat numbers, section labels, button text. Tight tracking on large sizes (`-0.5` to `-1`), wide tracking on small caps labels (`+1` to `+2`).
- **Body:** Inter — used for paragraphs, descriptions, message text.
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono — used only for redemption codes.

### Layout principles

- Dark theme is the default and only theme (no light mode in v1).
- Cards have generous padding (14–20px) and rounded corners (12–20px depending on hierarchy).
- Hero CTAs use the gold gradient (`gold → goldBright`) on dark backgrounds for maximum contrast.
- Section labels are 10pt, all caps, gold, letter-spaced — they function like chapter markers.
- Featured/PR content is bordered in `borderGold`; standard cards in `border`.
- Stats are *huge* (24–80pt display font) — the number is the hero.

### Iconography

Lucide React, 1.5–2px stroke. Sized 10–22px depending on context. Gold for active/featured, muted for inactive.

## 5. Screen-by-Screen Specification

### 5.1 Home

**Purpose:** Be the daily hook. The kid opens the app and sees points, streak, what's next, what's new from coach, and their latest PR.

**Sections (top to bottom):**

1. **Header** — "Welcome back" + first/last name in display caps. Bell icon top right (linked to messages, with unread dot).
2. **Points hero card** — gold gradient. Big point total, progress bar to next reward, "X pts away." Tap → Rewards.
3. **Stats row** (3 columns) — Streak (with flame icon), Rank (with trophy), Exit velo PR (with zap).
4. **NEW FROM COACH** — if there's an unwatched coach video, show it here as a large 3:4 thumbnail with COACH badge, duration, title, and a 2-line note preview. Tap → Message thread.
5. **UP NEXT** — next session card with date/time, session name, cage assignment, and a CONFIRMED pill.
6. **ASSIGNMENTS · X DUE** — top 2 pending assignments with point badges. "See all" → Work tab.
7. **LATEST PERSONAL BEST** — full-width hero card, gold "NEW PR · 68 MPH" badge, play button. Tap → Swing detail.

**Interaction priorities:**
- The points card is the most-tapped element by design. Friction to "what can I get?" must be one tap.
- Coach video, if present, jumps the line — it's the most important social/emotional hook.

### 5.2 My Videos (4 tabs)

**Purpose:** The athlete's complete video library. This is where they live when they're not actively training.

**Tabs:**

1. **SWINGS** — every swing captured by the cage system (HitTrax etc.), grouped by session OR shown as a flat grid of thumbnails. Toggle pills: BY SESSION / ALL SWINGS.
2. **HOMEWORK** — videos the athlete recorded and submitted as homework. Shows submitted date, coach rating (1–5 stars), and feedback excerpt. Status badges: REVIEWED.
3. **MESSAGES** — videos exchanged between athlete and coach (both directions). Shows COACH or YOU badge, label (SWING ANALYSIS / GAME FOOTAGE / DRILL DEMO / HOME PRACTICE / INSPIRATION), duration, title. Includes a prominent "SEND A VIDEO TO COACH" CTA at the top.
4. **FAVORITES** — swings the athlete has hearted. Best moments, PRs, anything they want to keep close.

**Stats bar (Swings tab only):** Total swings / PRs / Sessions, rendered in a single card with vertical dividers.

**Swing thumbnail design (3:4 aspect):**
- Gold-gradient background if PR, dark-gradient otherwise
- Centered play icon
- PR badge top-left if applicable
- Heart icon top-right if favorited
- Bottom strip with exit velo (large), launch angle, distance

**Session group (Swings → BY SESSION view):**
- Session header: date, time, name, swing count, avg/best velo, PR pill if any
- Grid of first 4 swing thumbnails (3 wide on phone)
- "View all N swings →" link

### 5.3 Session Detail

Header with session metadata. Stats card showing Swings / Avg / Best / PRs (Best and PRs in gold). Filter pills: All / PRs / Best / Contact. Grid of all swings from that session.

### 5.4 Swing Detail

**Top:** Back button, heart (toggle favorite), share button (→ Share Card screen).

**Hero:** 16:9 video player placeholder with PERSONAL BEST badge if applicable, swing number, time scrubber.

**Below hero:** Session metadata (date, time, session name).

**SWING METRICS** — 2x2 grid:
- Exit Velocity (with "New PR!" or delta vs avg)
- Launch Angle (with "Optimal" / "Adjust" verdict)
- Distance (with hit result: Home Run / Extra Base / Single / Out)
- Contact (Clean / Off, with "Hard hit" / "Try again" subtitle)

PR cells get gold border + PR badge + gold value text.

**Action row:** SHARE (outlined gold) + SEND TO COACH (filled gold), 50/50 split.

**If PR:**
- COACH MIKE'S NOTE card — italicized quote with coach avatar
- "+15 points earned" call-out card with sparkles icon

### 5.5 Share Card (Social)

**Purpose:** Generate a beautiful, brand-consistent 9:16 portrait card the parent or athlete can drop straight into Instagram Stories, TikTok, or text.

**Card composition (220px wide preview, full-bleed export at 1080x1920):**
- Dark gradient background (`dark → darker → darkest`)
- Gold accent lines top and bottom
- Top: "INFINITE HITTING" wordmark + "CLUBHOUSE · DALLAS N." location
- Middle: "NEW PERSONAL BEST" label + huge gold velo number (80pt+) + "MPH EXIT VELO" subtitle + Launch / Distance mini-stats with vertical divider
- Bottom: Player name + age group + date

**Below the card:**
- SHARE TO grid: Instagram / TikTok / Message / More — each as a tappable tile
- QUICK ACTIONS list: Save to Camera Roll / Copy Image / Send to Coach

**Implementation note:** The actual export should render at 1080x1920 (9:16) for Instagram/TikTok stories. The on-screen preview is a scaled-down version. Use `html2canvas` or server-side image rendering depending on platform.

### 5.6 Send Video to Coach

**Purpose:** Let athletes (or their parents on their behalf) send a video clip directly to the coach with context.

**Flow:**
1. Choose source — Record New (camera) or Upload (photos).
2. Tag — Game Footage / Home Practice / Swing Check / Question / Other.
3. Optional message field.
4. "Sent from" selector — Jake (you) / Dad / Mom (multi-user account context).
5. SEND TO COACH MIKE button (gold, full width, sticky bottom).
6. Success screen: green check, "SENT!", auto-navigate to message thread after 2s.

### 5.7 Message Thread (with Coach)

**Header:** Back / Coach avatar + name + Online indicator / blank.

**Bubble types:**
- **Text from coach** — left-aligned, dark card, cream text
- **Video from coach** — left-aligned, 3:4 thumbnail with COACH · LABEL badge top-left, duration bottom-right, gold border. Caption below.
- **Text from you** — right-aligned, gold background, dark text
- **Video from you** — right-aligned, 3:4 thumbnail with YOU · LABEL badge, gold border accent. Caption below right-aligned, often "From Dad · Sent yesterday."

**Composer bar (sticky bottom):** Camera icon → Send Video flow / Paperclip → Send Video flow / Text input / Send button (gold).

### 5.8 Assignments (Work tab)

**Purpose:** The homework queue. Coach assigns drills, kid completes them, points get awarded.

**Sections:**
- DUE THIS WEEK · N — pending assignments with target icon, title, duration, due day, point badge. Tap → Assignment Detail.
- COMPLETED — submitted/reviewed list with green check, "Awaiting review" or "Reviewed · +25 pts" status. Tap → Videos.

### 5.9 Assignment Detail

Header: Back / DUE [DAY] pill.

**Title block:** "ASSIGNMENT" label + assignment title in display caps + "Coach Mike · 15 min" + point badge.

**WATCH THE DRILL:** 16:9 video card with COACH DEMO badge.

**WHAT TO DO:** Numbered list inside a single card, each step in a row with a numbered circle and instruction text.

**Sticky CTA:** "RECORD YOUR ATTEMPT" with camera icon (gold).

### 5.10 Record Assignment

Camera viewport (placeholder rendered as gradient + camera icon + "CAMERA PREVIEW" label).
- Top-left: REC indicator with timer
- Bottom row: Upload (left) / Big record button (center, gold, with concentric ring shadow) / Flash (right)

**Submit flow:** tap record → loading → success screen with "+25" points display → auto-return to home after 3s.

### 5.11 Rewards

**Tabs:** Redeem / Earn / History.

**Redeem tab:**
- Points hero card (same gold gradient as home)
- Reward cards sorted by cost ascending
- Each card: icon tile / category label / name / cost (gold) / progress bar / "READY TO REDEEM" or "X PTS AWAY"
- If `points >= cost`: gold "REDEEM NOW →" CTA at bottom of card

**Earn tab:** List of earning rules with icons:
- Attend a session: +10
- Complete homework: +25
- New personal best: +15
- Streak milestone (every 7 days): +30
- Top 10 weekly leaderboard: +50
- Refer a friend: +100

**History tab:** Reverse-chronological list of points earned, each with action / date / point delta.

### 5.12 Reward Redeem

**Confirm step:** Reward icon / category / name. Card showing: Your points / Reward cost (red) / Balance after (gold). Sticky CTA: CONFIRM REDEMPTION.

**Code step:** Green check / "REDEEMED!" / "Show this at the front desk." White card with QR code (15x15 grid placeholder) + alphanumeric code in mono font (e.g., `IH-7F4K-92B`).

### 5.13 Training Library

Search/filter chips: All / Hitting / Mental / Situational / Fielding.

Featured hero: 16:10 video card with "NEW THIS WEEK" badge, duration · category, title.

CONTINUE WATCHING list: 80x60 thumbnails + title / duration · category · level.

### 5.14 Video Player

Standard player layout. Hero video (16:9). Below: category breadcrumb / title / duration · coach · view count.

### 5.15 Leaderboard

**Header:** "11U · EXIT VELO" / "LEADERBOARD" / "All locations · Updated today"

**Podium (top 3):** 2nd / 1st / 3rd visual with circular avatars, name initial, name first name, location, podium bar (1st is tallest and gold), rank number on the bar, value below.

**You card:** Gold-tinted card showing current rank, value, weekly change ("+3 ↑").

**TOP 25 list:** Rows with rank / avatar / name (with "(You)" suffix if self) / location / value. Self row gets gold tint and gold border.

### 5.16 Messages (list)

Standard inbox list. Each row: avatar (gold if unread) / from / time / preview (with video icon if video attached) / unread dot.

### 5.17 Profile

**Top:** Big avatar (gold tile, initial) / name / "11U · SS/2B · #7 · Infinite Hitting Dallas N."

**PERSONAL BESTS:** 2x2 grid: Exit Velo / Launch Angle / Distance / Contact Rate. Big gold numbers.

**ACHIEVEMENTS · 3/12:** 4-column badge grid. Earned badges show in gold; locked badges are dimmed.

**Settings list:** Settings / Notifications / Privacy.

## 6. Data Model (Conceptual)

This is a logical model, not a physical schema. Adapt to your DB of choice (Supabase/Postgres recommended given existing CKO Kickboxing platform stack).

### Core entities

```
Athlete
  id, first_name, last_name, age_group (8U..18U), position[], jersey_number
  primary_location_id, parent_user_ids[], date_of_birth
  point_balance (denormalized, derived from PointsLedger)
  streak_count, streak_last_date
  created_at, updated_at

Location
  id, name, slug, address, timezone, brand_id (for multi-tenant)

Coach
  id, first_name, last_name, location_ids[], avatar_url, bio

Session
  id, athlete_id, coach_id, location_id, scheduled_at, duration_min
  cage_number, session_type (hitting/group/assessment/etc.)
  status (scheduled/checked_in/completed/cancelled/no_show)
  capture_source (hittrax/rapsodo/manual/none)

Swing
  id, session_id, athlete_id, swing_number, captured_at
  exit_velocity_mph, launch_angle_deg, distance_ft
  contact_quality (clean/off), result (HR/2B+/1B/out)
  is_personal_best (denormalized; recompute on insert)
  is_favorite, video_url, thumbnail_url, raw_capture_payload (jsonb)

Assignment (homework)
  id, athlete_id, coach_id, title, description, due_at
  drill_video_url, instructions[], duration_min, point_reward
  status (pending/submitted/reviewed)
  created_at

AssignmentSubmission
  id, assignment_id, video_url, submitted_at, submitted_by_user_id
  coach_rating (1-5), coach_note, reviewed_at

VideoMessage
  id, thread_id, sender_user_id, sender_type (coach/athlete/parent)
  recipient_user_id, video_url, thumbnail_url, duration_sec
  label (swing_analysis/game_footage/drill_demo/home_practice/inspiration/question)
  caption, sent_at, read_at

MessageThread
  id, athlete_id, coach_id, last_message_at, unread_count_athlete, unread_count_coach

Reward
  id, name, category (apparel/sessions/equipment/experiences)
  cost_points, description, image_url, available_locations[]
  inventory_count (nullable for digital), active

Redemption
  id, athlete_id, reward_id, redemption_code (e.g. IH-7F4K-92B)
  redeemed_at, fulfilled_at, fulfilled_by_user_id, points_spent

PointsLedger
  id, athlete_id, delta (signed integer), reason
  reason_type (session_attended/homework_completed/pr_hit/streak_milestone/leaderboard_top10/referral/redemption/manual_adjust)
  related_entity_type, related_entity_id, created_at, created_by

Achievement
  id, key, name, description, icon, criteria (jsonb)

AthleteAchievement
  athlete_id, achievement_id, earned_at

LeaderboardSnapshot
  id, age_group, metric (exit_velo/distance/etc.), scope (location/region/national)
  generated_at, entries (jsonb: [{athlete_id, rank, value}])

User (auth — many-to-many with Athlete via parent_user_ids)
  id, email, phone, role (athlete/parent/coach/admin), auth_provider_id
```

### Derived / computed views

- **Athlete dashboard view:** point balance, streak, current rank, latest PR, next session, pending assignments count. Compute on read or maintain as a materialized view refreshed on relevant events.
- **Session summary:** swing count, avg/min/max exit velo, PR flag.
- **Personal bests:** Top swing per metric (velo / distance / launch_angle in optimal range).

## 7. Key User Flows

### 7.1 The "I just hit a PR" flow (the magic moment)

1. Athlete swings in cage. HitTrax captures velo = 68 mph.
2. Backend ingests swing data (webhook or polling). Computes `is_personal_best = true` because previous best was 65.
3. PointsLedger entry created: `+15, reason=pr_hit, related=swing_id`.
4. Push notification fires: "🔥 New PR! 68 mph — your best ever."
5. Athlete opens app → Home shows "LATEST PERSONAL BEST" hero card.
6. Tap → Swing Detail shows all 4 metrics, PR badge, gold borders, coach note (if coach has marked it up), "+15 points earned" card.
7. Tap SHARE → Share Card screen with auto-generated 9:16 portrait. Tap Instagram → handoff to Stories.
8. Tap SEND TO COACH → pre-filled video send flow with this swing attached.

### 7.2 The "homework completed" flow

1. Coach assigns drill via coach app. Athlete gets push: "New assignment from Coach Mike."
2. Athlete opens Work tab → Assignment Detail → watches coach demo.
3. Tap RECORD YOUR ATTEMPT → camera screen → records → submits.
4. Backend stores submission, sets assignment status = submitted.
5. PointsLedger: `+25, reason=homework_completed`.
6. Coach reviews via coach app, leaves rating + note. Status → reviewed.
7. Athlete gets push: "Coach Mike reviewed your homework!" → opens Videos → Homework tab.

### 7.3 The "parent sends game footage" flow

1. Parent (signed in to athlete's account, role=parent) opens app at the field.
2. Tap Videos → Messages → SEND A VIDEO TO COACH.
3. Records 18s clip from the stands. Tags as "Game Footage." Caption: "First extra base hit of the season!"
4. "Sent from: Mom" (the parent context is preserved).
5. Coach receives in their thread. Replies with text or video. Both bubbles appear in athlete's Messages tab and in the coach message thread.

### 7.4 The "redemption" flow

1. Athlete hits 400 points → can redeem an Infinite Hitting Tee.
2. Rewards → tap card → Confirm screen shows balance math.
3. Confirm → backend creates Redemption row, generates code (`IH-7F4K-92B`), debits PointsLedger (`-400, reason=redemption`).
4. Code screen shown with QR + alphanumeric.
5. Athlete shows phone at front desk. Front desk app scans QR → marks redemption fulfilled, hands over tee.

## 8. Engineering Notes

### Suggested stack (aligning with existing FITT/MyJSC OS work)

- **Frontend:** React Native (or Expo) for native iOS/Android; React web for parent/coach companion if needed
- **Backend:** Node.js / TypeScript, Postgres (Supabase) for primary data, Redis for leaderboard caching
- **Auth:** Supabase Auth or Clerk; multi-role (athlete/parent/coach/admin) with athlete-parent linkage
- **Storage:** Supabase Storage or S3 for video assets; CDN in front (Cloudflare/CloudFront)
- **Video pipeline:** Mux or Cloudflare Stream for adaptive playback + thumbnails; raw capture from cage systems goes to S3 → transcoded → CDN
- **Push:** Expo Push or OneSignal
- **Capture integrations:** HitTrax/Rapsodo webhooks if available; otherwise polling adapters or a small on-prem agent at each location that syncs to backend
- **Payments:** Stripe Connect (already in flight as part of broader Infinite Hitting / FITT Space strategy)
- **CRM/automation:** GoHighLevel hooked in via the two-tier executor (API where supported, browser agent fallback) — this is the existing MyJSC OS pattern

### Multi-tenancy

The Athlete App is built for Infinite Hitting first but should be white-labelable. Brand tokens (colors, logos, copy) should be loaded per-tenant from a `Brand` table. Don't hard-code "Infinite Hitting" anywhere except as the default tenant.

### Real-time

Leaderboards, message threads, and the points balance all benefit from real-time updates. Use Supabase Realtime or a thin WebSocket layer. Avoid full polling.

### Offline / poor connectivity

Cages are often in metal buildings with bad cell. Cache the athlete's last 30 days of swings and homework videos locally. Defer outbound video uploads until on wifi (with a clear "queued" indicator).

### Privacy / minor safety

- Athletes are minors. All accounts should require a parent email at signup.
- Coach↔athlete messaging must always be visible to the parent (no DM-only-with-kid mode).
- Video uploads from athletes should be screened for inappropriate content (use a moderation API, e.g., Hive, AWS Rekognition).
- Leaderboards should show first name + last initial publicly, not full names. Full name visible only within the athlete's home location.
- Comply with COPPA. No third-party ads. No data sales.

### Accessibility

- Minimum tap target 44x44.
- Sufficient contrast (the gold-on-dark palette has been chosen with WCAG AA in mind for primary text; verify on actual devices).
- Support Dynamic Type / system font scaling.
- VoiceOver labels on all interactive elements.

## 10. Success Metrics

### Engagement
- D1 / D7 / D30 retention
- Sessions per active athlete per week
- Median time-to-open after a session ends (target: <2 hours)
- Share card generation rate (target: >20% of PRs)

### Business
- App-attributed renewal rate vs. non-app-attributed
- Average reward redemption per athlete per quarter
- Net point balance growth (proxy for engagement)
- Parent-initiated coach video messages per week (proxy for parent value)

### Product health
- Swing ingestion latency (cage → app, target p50 <5 min, p95 <15 min)
- Video upload success rate
- Push notification CTR
- Crash-free sessions >99.5%

## 11. Open Questions

1. **HitTrax integration:** Is there a documented webhook/API, or is this an on-prem scrape job?
2. **Capture-to-video pipeline:** Does HitTrax export video files, or only metrics? If metrics only, do we capture video separately (overhead camera per cage)?
3. **Multi-athlete households:** A family with 2 kids both at Infinite Hitting — do parents see a unified inbox, or switch between profiles?
4. **Coach assignment of points:** Can coaches award bonus points manually, or only through the rule engine?
5. **Reward fulfillment:** Front desk hands over physical merch — but who manages inventory? Does the app surface low-stock warnings to the location?
6. **Leaderboard scope toggles:** v1 ships with national 11U exit velo. Do we ship location/regional toggles in v1, or wait?
7. **GoHighLevel role:** Is GHL the source of truth for athletes (synced into the app), or vice versa?
8. **White-label timing:** When do we lift this for the second vertical SaaS deployment? That date drives how aggressively we abstract the brand layer in v1.

## 13. Glossary

- **PR** — Personal Record. A new best on a given metric (most often exit velocity).
- **Exit velo** — exit velocity, the speed of the ball off the bat. Measured in mph.
- **Launch angle** — vertical angle of the ball off the bat. Optimal range is roughly 10°–25°.
- **Cage** — an enclosed batting area, typically with a pitching machine and a capture system.
- **HitTrax / Rapsodo / Blast** — capture systems used in baseball/softball facilities. HitTrax does ball-flight tracking; Rapsodo handles pitching/hitting analytics; Blast is a bat sensor.
- **Front toss / tee work** — common drill formats. Front toss is a coach gently tossing balls from in front; tee work is hitting off a stationary tee.
- **11U / 12U / etc.** — age groups in youth baseball. 11U means 11-and-under.
