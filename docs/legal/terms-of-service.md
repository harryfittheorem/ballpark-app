## 1. The service

Ballpark is a mobile app operated by Fit Theorem ("we", "us", "Ballpark") that families use to interact with a youth sports performance facility ("the facility") where their kid trains. Today the pilot facility is Infinite Hitting; over time additional facility franchises will be onboarded as separate tenants in the same app.

Through Ballpark, a parent can:

- Create a family account and add a kid profile.
- Book private lessons, group classes, and cage rentals with coaches at their home facility.
- Receive short coaching video messages from their kid's coach.
- Receive drill assignments from the coach, mark them done, and earn points.
- Track points, redeem rewards, view a facility leaderboard, and review order history.

The service depends on the facility — bookings, coaching, drills, and reward fulfillment are delivered by the facility's coaches and staff. We provide the software.

## 4. Coach video content guidelines

Coach-to-family video messages and drill videos are intended for instructional and coaching purposes. By recording or uploading a video through Ballpark, the coach (or, where applicable, the family submitting a drill response in a future release) agrees that:

- The content is coaching or instructional in nature and relates to the recipient kid's training.
- The content does not include third-party copyrighted footage (for example, broadcast game footage) that the coach does not have the right to share.
- The content complies with the facility's code of conduct.
- The kid being filmed (if any) and that kid's parent have consented, as required by the facility's policies.

We may remove video content that violates these guidelines.

## 5. Points and rewards

The points system inside Ballpark is a loyalty / engagement feature. By using it you agree:

- **Points have no cash value.** They are not money, are not redeemable for cash, and are not transferable between kids, families, or accounts.
- Points may be **adjusted, revoked, or reset** by the facility or by us in the case of error, abuse, fraud, account closure, or similar circumstances.
- Reward availability, point costs, and the catalogue of products are set by the facility and may change at any time. A reward shown today may not be available tomorrow.
- A redemption code generated for a reward is a pickup token honored by the facility front desk. Lost or shared codes are the user's responsibility.
- Points may expire if your account is inactive for an extended period. Specific expiration rules will be posted in the app once finalized.

## 6. Bookings and cancellation

Bookings made through Ballpark are bookings with **the facility**, not with Ballpark. We provide the software the facility uses to schedule the session.

- Cancellation deadlines, no-show fees, refund eligibility, and rescheduling rules are set by the facility and may differ between facilities.
- To change or cancel a booking today, contact the facility front desk. In-app cancellation and reschedule controls will be added in a future release; until then the facility's posted policies govern.
- Time slots, coach availability, cage availability, and session types are determined by the facility.
