Infinite Hitting | App & Dashboard Requirements
Consolidated product development notes • November 13, 2026 — with delivery status
Objective: A unified platform for franchisor oversight, franchise owner performance, marketing, retention, training, accountability, and proactive support.

1. Franchisor & Owner Dashboards ✅ Complete

Location Performance Tracker ✅ — Location performance table on the HQ dashboard with Leads, Evaluations, Closing Rate, New Members, MRR, and Month-to-Date Sales per location.
Filtering and comparison ✅ — Sorting by any KPI, network totals, and location-to-location comparison via leaderboards and performance history.
Network MRR ✅ — Network MRR shown across all locations with network average and month-over-month change indicators.
Network churn ✅ — Network Churn & LTV section in HQ dashboard with location-level breakdown.

2. Leaderboards & Operational Rankings ✅ Complete

Clickable categories ✅ — HQ Leaderboards page with selectable metric tabs — click any category to rank all locations by that KPI with network benchmark comparison.
Commercial and retention metrics ✅ — MRR, Active Members, New Members MTD, Eval Closing Rate, Retention Rate — all rankable in the leaderboard.
Other rankings ✅ — Daily Checklist Completion and Avg Google Rating included as rankable metrics on the leaderboard.

3. Franchise Owner Profiles & Journey ✅ Complete

Owner record ✅ — HQ Owner Profiles page with one profile per owner showing location, contact info, franchise signing/opening dates, MRR, and member count.
Call notes ✅ — Chronological call notes from Marketing, Sales, and Leadership — filterable by department with add-note capability.
Action tracking ✅ — Assigned tasks with deadlines, responsible parties, and status (Open / Done / Overdue) — overdue items flagged with alerts.
Monthly performance history ✅ — 6-month MRR, New Members, Leads, Closing Rate, and Churn per owner compared against network averages with percentage delta.
Location-age benchmarking ✅ — Network average benchmark row shown alongside monthly performance history for direct comparison.

4. Google Drive Document Integration 🔗 Pending Client Setup

Owner-specific library — Attach, link or sync each owner's Drive folders/files: franchise agreements, onboarding, training, marketing, sales, operations, financials and meeting notes.
Document tools — Search, categorization, version history, new-file syncing, owner/location associations and role-based permissions.
Awaiting Google Workspace OAuth credentials from client to proceed with integration.

5. Automated Revenue Decline Intervention ✅ Complete

Trigger ✅ — After two consecutive completed months of month-over-month revenue decline, send an in-app notification to the affected franchise owner.
Call booking ✅ — Prompt a performance review and include a direct corporate booking link.
Corporate workflow ✅ — Notify leadership, log alert on owner journey, track booking/completion and remind if no appointment is made.

6. Marketing Tab & Events ✅ Complete

Navigation ✅ — Marketing added to the left sidebar of both Franchisor and Franchise Owner dashboards.
Calendar ✅ — Marketing calendar with corporate events, campaigns, meetings, deadlines, and color-coded event categories.
Ads Manager data ✅ — Ads Manager tab showing Ad Spend, Leads, Cost per Lead, Evals Booked, Cost per Eval, New Members, CAC, and ROAS.
Permissions ✅ — Franchise owner view scoped to their location's advertising data.
Resources ✅ — Resources tab in Marketing with marketing materials, campaign assets, and promotional content.

7. Retention Tab ✅ Complete

Core metrics ✅ — Nationwide Churn Rate and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV); Average Retention removed.
Drill-down ✅ — Click metrics for detailed views; switch between nationwide and individual locations; compare with network benchmarks and chart historical trends.
Date controls ✅ — Month, quarter, year and custom date ranges.

8. Trainual Training Integration 🔗 Pending Client Setup

Training navigation — Training tab in both dashboards with Trainual access to onboarding, SOPs, courses and playbooks.
Monitoring — Track training progress and completion for owners, GMs and instructors; corporate network oversight and role-based visibility.
Access — Single sign-on or seamless direct launch where technically supported.
Awaiting Trainual account + API/SSO access from client to proceed with integration.

9. App Activity & Engagement ✅ Complete

Per-user analytics ✅ — HQ Engagement page shows last active, logins (30d), avg session, checklist completion %, and Trainual progress per user.
Franchisor oversight ✅ — Filterable by role (Owner / GM / Instructor) and status (Active / Warning / Inactive) across all locations.
Alerts ✅ — Alert banner for users inactive 14+ days; composite engagement score (0–100) based on logins, checklists, Trainual, and session time; status badges at 7d / 14d / 30d thresholds.
Governance ✅ — HQ-only access; engagement score methodology documented in-app for transparency.

10. Player Profiles — Re-Evaluation Tracking ✅ Complete

Dedicated Re-Eval tab ✅ — Re-Evaluation tab added to each player profile.
Completed count ✅ — Total number of re-evaluations completed by the player is displayed.
Historical log ✅ — Each re-evaluation date, results, and coach notes are recorded.
Progress tracking ✅ — Outcomes compared between re-evaluations to visualize player improvement over time.
Upcoming re-evaluation ✅ — Next scheduled re-evaluation date shown when available.
Role access ✅ — Authorized coaches, GMs, and corporate users can review re-evaluation history.
