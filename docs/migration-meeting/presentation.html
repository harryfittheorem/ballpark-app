05 / A familiar approach—with clear safeguards

Rehearse. Reconcile.
Switch only when ready.

Two vendor playbooks · not customer case studies

HubSpot CRM guidance: map and clean data, rehearse away from production, validate users and reports, train, then stabilize.

Stripe payment guidance: transfer saved methods securely; recreate subscriptions separately; preserve billing dates and review future schedules.

Rehearse→Reconcile→Approve→Switch cohort→Monitor
Approval requested

Authorize discovery and a gated Clearwater pilot only.

  • Weekly test results: pass/fail, open issues and named owners. Staff signoff before shadow.
  • Shadow week: daily comparison of bookings, balances, expected dues and reports.
  • Owners can hold launch; a critical issue or unexplained variance means “not yet.”
  • Separate approved billing handoff only; no-card-re-entry gate must pass.
  • Old autopay/retries stopped before new collection. No automatic shutdown or network rollout.
