# Ballpark — Leave Behind

**The operating system for youth sports franchises.**

---

## The Problem

Youth sports facilities run on tools built for yoga studios — MindBody, Square, Excel, Facebook groups. The moment a kid walks out the door, the relationship is gone. Multiply that across a franchise: 5 locations, 15 coaches, 400 families, zero visibility, $300-$500 per churned family per year.

---

## The Product

A mobile app built around the athlete — not the front desk.

- **Home dashboard** — Kid's name, points (847), rank (#3 in 14U), coach video, next booking, pending drill, personal best. All live. A reason to open the app every day.
- **HitTrax auto-import** — Exit velocity, launch angle, distance, session stats. Appears in the app 2 minutes after the kid leaves the cage. No coach data entry. No parent upload.
- **Two-way video messaging** — Coach sends a swing breakdown. Parent replies with a backyard video. Full Mux HLS pipeline. Relationship between sessions, not just during them.
- **Drill assignments** — Coach assigns homework with a video, due date, and point reward. Parent submits. Coach reviews with stars + feedback. Points credited. Loop closed.
- **Gamification engine** — Points for sessions, drills, PRs, achievements. Redeemable for lessons, gear, or store items. Live leaderboard by age group. Kids compete. Kids stay.
- **Booking** — Filter by type, pick a slot, confirm in under 20 seconds. Confirmation email via Resend. 40%+ of bookings shift from phone to app.

---

## Why Not MindBody?

| | MindBody | Ballpark |
|---|---|---|
| Built for | Owner / front desk | The athlete |
| HitTrax / sensor data | None | Automated, 2-min lag |
| Franchise OS | Per-location only | Multi-tenant, one view |
| Kid engagement | Login screen | Daily reason to open |
| Points economy | No | Full gamification loop |

## The June Pilot

**Location:** One Infinite Hitting facility — 40-60 families, 4-6 coaches.

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | 10 families, 2 coaches. Invite-only soft launch. |
| Weeks 2-3 | All coaches. 30-40 families. HitTrax live. First redemptions. |
| Week 4 | Full location. Email blast. Front desk QR scanner active. |

**We measure:** ≥3 app opens/kid/week · ≥40% bookings via app · ≥60% drill completion · <24h coach response · ≥10 redemptions · NPS ≥8 · <2% crash rate.

**We own:** Coach onboarding (10 min/coach), family invite emails, week-1 live support, weekly data recaps to IH leadership.
