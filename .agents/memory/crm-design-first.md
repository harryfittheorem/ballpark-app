name: CRM design-first scope
description: Creator's design-first requirement and requested native CRM capabilities.
---

The creator wants to finalize the native CRM design before building it. They requested shared messaging threads, opportunity pipelines and configurable milestone/date-based workflows inspired by GoHighLevel and newer offerings. Tagging must support both manual changes and workflow-driven changes, with communication targeted by tags.

**Why:** The creator explicitly requested design and scope review first, including these capabilities; a basic contact list or fixed reminder toggles would not satisfy that direction.

**How to apply:** Preserve these capabilities in design and scope discussions. Design approval is not permission to connect providers, send messages or build all proposed phases. Keep tentative provider/account choices distinct from confirmed product requirements.
