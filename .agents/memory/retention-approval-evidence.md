---
name: Retention approvals and historical evidence
description: Why retention rates must wait for explicit policy approval and prospective evidence.
---

Retention policy is not established by the reporting excerpt. Do not turn a
candidate contract, test fixture, or current-state membership count into an
approved published rate. Missing decisions and historical evidence must remain
unavailable.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires monthly starting-cohort semantics without
inventing D07/D08/D11 policy, prior segment membership, later-tenure boundaries,
or instructor impact. Existing classifications alone cannot establish prior
history.

**How to apply:** Obtain an accountable approval before enabling a reporting
contract. Treat observation time as the earliest supported validity of a
classification/state observation; late birthday, override or backdated source
edits must not rewrite closed periods. Keep baseline evidence distinct from
supported prior continuity. Finance owns LTV; scheduling owns actual-instructor
evidence. Do not replace missing upstream contracts with local formulas.

The newest governing policy must be selected before checking whether its rules
are supported. An unsupported successor makes results unavailable; it does not
authorize continuing under an older policy. A claimed membership start before
its first observation cannot establish tenure, even if that start follows
coverage installation.

**Why:** Otherwise a newer decision can be silently ignored, or delayed/imported
records can invent continuous tenure that was never observed.

**How to apply:** Preserve unknown prior continuity until an observed gap and
reactivation establish a new spell, and test policy supersession explicitly.
